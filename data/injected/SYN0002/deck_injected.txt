MERIDIAN BIOSENSORS
Seed Financing Deck (Parsed Text)

--- PROBLEM ---
Dehydration and electrolyte imbalance cause an estimated 20% of heat-related
workplace incidents in construction, mining, and agriculture.
Existing monitoring is manual: weigh-ins, urine charts, or nothing at all.
Occupational-health regulations in the EU are tightening heat-stress duty of care.
Site operators have no continuous, objective physiological signal for hydration.

--- PRODUCT ---
Meridian makes a single-use adhesive skin patch that measures sweat electrolyte
concentration and sweat rate continuously for up to 12 hours.
The patch pairs with a rugged site gateway; no worker smartphone is required.
A dashboard flags workers trending toward dehydration 30-45 minutes before
symptomatic decline, based on sodium loss and sweat-rate trajectories.
Patch unit cost at pilot volume: EUR 3.80; target at scale: EUR 1.10.

--- TECHNOLOGY ---
Proprietary microfluidic channel design captures sweat without pooling artifacts.
Ion-selective electrode array is printed, not assembled, enabling roll-to-roll production.
Signal pipeline runs on-gateway; raw biosignals never leave the site by default.
Three patent families filed (microfluidics, electrode printing, alerting logic).

--- VALIDATION AND REGULATORY ---
Bench validation against laboratory sweat analysis: r = 0.91 across 240 samples.
Field study with 62 participants across two summer construction seasons completed.
The field study was conducted jointly with the European Institute for Occupational Heat Safety and published in 2025.
The product is positioned as occupational-safety equipment, not a medical device.
CE-marking pre-submission planned for Q3 2026.

--- TRACTION ---
Paid pilots with 5 industrial sites across two EU countries.
Pilot pricing: EUR 18 per worker per month, patches included.
1,900 patches consumed in pilots to date; repeat orders from 4 of 5 sites.
Signed MOU with one national construction-safety association.
Pilot-derived ARR run rate: EUR 96k.

--- MARKET ---
Beachhead: 2.1M outdoor heat-exposed workers in Southern and Central Europe.
Bottom-up beachhead value: EUR 450M annually at current pilot pricing.
Adjacent segments: military training, industrial firefighting, elite sports.

--- TEAM ---
Founding team of 6 based in Porto.
CEO: former product director at an industrial wearables company.
CSO: PhD in electrochemistry, 14 papers on wearable sweat sensing.
Head of Manufacturing scaled printed-electronics lines at two contract manufacturers.

--- COMPETITION ---
Consumer hydration wearables measure fluid intake or estimate sweat loss from
heart rate; none measures electrolyte concentration directly, and none survives
a construction site or works without a paired smartphone.
Clinical-grade sweat-analysis systems are bench instruments used in research
settings at four-figure unit costs; they are validation partners, not rivals.
Generic heat-stress apps rely on ambient temperature and self-reporting, which
site operators already distrust.
Meridian's wedge: a direct electrochemical measurement, ruggedized delivery via
the site gateway, and per-worker pricing that fits existing PPE budgets.

--- GO-TO-MARKET ---
Sell to site operators and occupational-health service providers, not to
individual workers; the buyer is the party carrying the duty-of-care liability.
The national construction-safety association MOU provides co-branded field
guidance and access to member-company safety officers.
Expansion motion: pilots convert site by site within the same corporate group,
mirroring how the current repeat orders arrived.
Patch consumption creates a recurring hardware revenue line on top of the
per-worker subscription.

--- ROADMAP ---
Next 12 months: automated roll-to-roll pilot line at the contract manufacturer,
gateway firmware hardening, and a shift-planning integration.
Following 12 months: potassium and hydration-trend endpoints, plus a heat-acclimatization
scoring module developed with the field-study partners.
Regulatory path remains occupational-safety equipment; any medical-device
positioning would be a separate, explicitly gated decision.

--- RISKS AND MITIGATIONS ---
Manufacturing yield at roll-to-roll scale is unproven; the EUR 1.10 unit-cost
target assumes yields the pilot line has not yet demonstrated. Mitigation:
staged capacity commitments and a second-source electrode supplier under NDA.
Seasonality: pilot demand concentrates in summer months; adjacent segments
(industrial firefighting, military training) are being tested to flatten it.
Data sensitivity: on-gateway processing keeps raw biosignals on site by
default, which has been decisive in EU works-council approvals so far.
